Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026 · App: My Business

My Business (“the app”) is provided by Gold Life LLC, doing business as Evolved by GL (“we,” “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: we don’t collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit your personal information to ourselves or any third party. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — customers, estimates, invoices, receipts, photos, and your company profile — is stored on your device and synced through your own private Apple iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). It is accessible only to you, on your devices signed in to your Apple ID. We have no access to it and cannot see it.

Camera

If you scan a receipt, the app uses your device camera on-device only. Images are stored with your data in your private iCloud and are never sent to us.

Subscriptions & payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details, card numbers, or billing information. Apple’s privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

The app is intended for business use and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions? Email evolvedbygl@icloud.com or visit evolvedbygl.com.
